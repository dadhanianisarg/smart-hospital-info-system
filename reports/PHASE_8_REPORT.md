## 4. Manuscript Structure (`paper/main.tex`)

The paper follows standard IEEE conference formatting:
- **Title:** *Patient-Conditioned Drug-Interaction Risk Prediction with Relational Graph Neural Networks and Cold-Start Generalization, Deployed as a Prescription-Time Check in a Hospital Information System*
- **Authors:** Yash Khose (23BCE0625) & Tanish Chawla (23BCE0502), School of Computer Science and Engineering, Vellore Institute of Technology.
- **Section I (Introduction):** Clinical polypharmacy hazards, physiological context dependence (Lisinopril + Ibuprofen with CKD), limitations of isolated edge prediction and transductive topological starvation, three primary contributions.
- **Section II (Related Work):** GNNs in biomedicine (Decagon, KGNN, CASTER), molecular representations (Morgan fingerprints, SMILES), clinical decision support integration.
- **Section III (Methodology):** Mathematical derivations for molecular projection, R-GCN message passing, inductive residual skip connections, scaled dot-product attention over clinical neighborhoods, and the pairwise MLP decoder.
- **Section IV (Experimental Setup):** CPU-only constraint (`torch.set_num_threads(8)`), 5-seed protocol, benchmark dataset characteristics, negative sampling disjointness, split protocols (S1, S2, S3).
- **Section V (Results and Discussion):** Link prediction analysis, Wilcoxon signed-rank significance tests, detailed ablation study.
- **Section VI (Calibration and Operational Performance):** L-BFGS temperature scaling ($T=2.10$), attention case study interpretability, 1,000-run latency benchmarking ($p50 = 0.50$\,ms, $p95 = 0.84$\,ms), full-stack HIS integration with non-blocking fail-safe architecture.
- **Section VII (Limitations and Governance):** Open-world negative sampling assumption, synthetic context rule bounds, PhysioNet MIMIC-IV DUA credentialing requirements.
- **Section VIII (Conclusion):** Summary of contributions and clinical utility.
- **References:** 12 peer-reviewed IEEE, Nature, BMJ, and Bioinformatics citations.
